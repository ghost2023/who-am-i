Who Am I?

Everyone can see your character. Except you.

Players

Timer

How to play

One by one: pass the phone to whoever's turn it is. They hold it up facing everyone else — screen out, don't peek — and ask yes/no questions until they guess who they are.

Group race: pass the phone around during the reveal so everyone learns everyone else's character (except their own). Then ask around the room — first to guess theirs wins.

Works offline. Add to home screen.
